RX/TX PCB build — price & assembly considerations
================================================
Saved: 2026-08-25
Context: Need working RX + TX before campus (leave ~2026-09-14). Budget hope ~$200 total.
Ballpark only — confirm with live JLCPCB / PCBWay quotes after Gerbers + BOM exist.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GOAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Both RX and TX boards ready to order and (ideally) tested before Sept 14.
- Prefer assembled boards over DIY for this deadline (QFN/TQFN EPAD parts:
  CP2102C, MAX97220A are hard with iron-only).
- Hot plate / full reflow kit: defer to winter break; not worth buying if
  it stays home unused during the school year.
- Optional later: portable iron for campus (small, useful for jacks/rewire).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DIY vs ASSEMBLED (why assembled for this deadline)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
~20 days until campus. RX PCB + TX schematic/PCB + fab/ship + bring-up
already fills the calendar. DIY adds a hot plate (~$25–60), paste/tweezers
(~$25–45), and 1–2 days soldering with risk of dead EPAD boards.

Assembled wins when: deadline matters, gear would sit unused, only need a
few good boards. DIY wins later when home for break and iterating.

Altoids-ish form factor (~95x60x21 mm tin sizing, not an actual tin):
aim ~85x55 mm board max; edge-mount USB-C + 3.5 mm / TRRS; pot shaft
through lid on RX.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PASSIVES / HAND SOLDER (if ever DIY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Use 0805 HandSolder footprints for all R/C (not 0201/0603 for first build).
- Iron-friendly: 0805, SOT-23, SOT-223 (LM3940), WROOM module, TSSOP (PCM5102).
- Needs reflow/hot plate: CP2102C (QFN+EP), MAX97220A (TQFN+EP).
- Schematic still had mixed 0201 vs 0805 footprints — standardize to 0805
  HandSolder before layout freeze.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHAT "TURNKEY" MEANS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Turnkey = fab house makes PCB + buys parts + stencil + SMT assembly + ships
finished boards.

Consigned = you buy parts and send them to the factory.

Want: turnkey (JLCPCB Economic PCBA).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. MOQ — YOU ARE NOT STUCK AT 5+5 ASSEMBLED
--------------------------------------------------------------------
JLCPCB:
  - Bare PCB promo often shows qty 5 and "fab from $2".
  - Economic PCBA order volume: 2–50 pcs (official capabilities page).
  - "Start from just 2 boards" on their assembly page.
  - Qty 2 is under SMT Assembly / Economic PCBA — toggle SMT ON in the
    quote flow. Fab-only UI may still default to 5.

PCBWay:
  - Assembly MOQ can be as low as 1 (setup still applies).
  - Usually more expensive for this prototype use case than JLCPCB.

Practical plan: 2 RX + 2 TX assembled (enough for bring-up + spare each).
Bump to 5 of a design only if the quote delta is small and you want spares.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. JLCPCB vs PCBWAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
JLCPCB: best when BOM is LCSC in-stock; ~$8 setup + cheap joints; Extended
        part fees ~$3/type for non-Basic ICs.
PCBWay: higher setup floor; better if you need DigiKey/Mouser sourcing.

For ~$200 budget: use JLCPCB. PCBWay only if critical ICs missing from LCSC.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. COUPONS / "FREE ASSEMBLY" (does not mean free boards)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Promo language ("free assembly for 1–6 layer, save $8 setup", monthly ~$25
in SMT coupons: $6 / $9 / $10) typically waives or discounts the SETUP fee.

You still pay:
  - Components (largest cost)
  - Stencil (~$1.50)
  - Extended-part / feeder fees
  - Shipping

Expect coupons to save ~$15–25 across two designs in one month — helpful,
not transformative.

Note: some $10 PCBA coupons require EasyEDA-exported files. KiCad Gerbers
usually use the generic $6/$9 SMT coupons — check Account → Coupons at
checkout.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
8. BALLPARK COSTS (USD, mid 2026, confirm with quote)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Assumptions: 2-layer (or simple 4-layer), single-sided SMT, ~65 placements
on RX, TX lighter (reuse pwr/ESP; PCM1808 + TRRS; no amp/pot/MAX97220),
turnkey, US shipping, optional pot left for hand-solder.

Plan A — 5 RX + 5 TX assembled
  Mid: ~$280–380  |  High: ~$400+
  Per board if split: roughly $30–40 range all-in

Plan B — 2 RX + 2 TX assembled  (TARGET for ~$200)
  Mid: ~$160–230  |  With setup coupons: closer to ~$150–210
  Parts dominate; TX cheaper than RX (~60–70% of RX parts)

Plan C — 5 RX + 2 TX
  Mid: ~$200–270

DIY first time (5 bare each + parts + budget hot plate gear)
  Often ~$200–320 out of pocket before yield risk; worse under deadline.

Rough JLCPCB line items for ONE design @ qty 2:
  Fab:            $5–15
  Setup:          $0–8 (coupon may zero)
  Stencil:        ~$1.50
  Extended fees:  $20–40 (ESP, UART bridge, audio ICs, connectors…)
  Components:     $25–50 (RX higher)
  Shipping:       share across designs if one shipment ~$25–40 total

Wildcards that raise price:
  - MAX97220A / PCM1808 / CP2102C not in LCSC stock
  - Through-hole pot assembled by factory
  - Double-sided SMT
  - Express shipping

--------------------------------------------------------------------
9. HOW TO STAY NEAR $200
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Qty 2 each (not 5+5) unless quote says otherwise.
2. All passives = JLCPCB "Basic" 0805.
3. Check LCSC stock BEFORE layout freeze: ESP32-WROOM-32U, CP2102C,
   MAX97220A, PCM5102, PCM1808, LM3940, USB-C, jacks.
4. Leave RX pot unpopulated (hand-solder one TH part later) if it saves
   hand-assembly fees.
5. Single combined shipment; no reflow gear purchase before campus.
6. Quote qty 2 and qty 5 once BOM exists; pick the cheaper path to
   "enough working boards".
