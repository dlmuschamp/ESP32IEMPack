* Purpose

What *type* of capacitor to buy for each RX schematic location --- ceramic vs
polarized --- so the BOM matches the datasheets. Values match
=KiCad/RX_SKETCHES/= as of 2026-08-25; re-check refs if you renumber parts.

Package default for hand-built boards: *0805* (or the HandSolder footprints
already on the symbols). Voltage rating: use *≥6.3 V* on 3.3 V rails; *≥10 V*
is safer. Prefer *X7R* (or X5R) for ceramics unless noted.

* Quick rule

| Use | Type |
|-----+------|
| Charge-pump flying / hold caps | *Always ceramic* (never electrolytic) |
| HF bypass next to IC pins (0.1 uF) | *Ceramic* |
| Input coupling / small filters | *Ceramic* |
| Large bulk on 3.3 V where a =+= is drawn | *Polarized OK* (tantalum / electrolytic) *or* ceramic MLCC |
| LM3940 =OUT= 33 uF | *Tantalum (or Al + tant)* with ESR in the LM3940 band --- not a random ultra-low-ESR ceramic |

* DAC sheet (=dac=) --- PCM5102

| Ref | Value | Buy | Why / where |
|-----+-------+-----+-------------|
| =C12= | 10 uF | *Polarized* (=Device:C_Polarized= on sheet) *or* 10 uF ceramic | Bulk on DVDD/AVDD/CPVDD to GND. =+= to *+3V3* if polarized. |
| =C13=, =C16=, =C17=, =C19= | 0.1 uF | *Ceramic* | Local HF bypass (supplies / LDOO). |
| =C14= | 2.2 uF | *Ceramic* | CAPP--CAPM flying cap. *Not* polarized. |
| =C15= | 2.2 uF | *Ceramic* | VNEG to GND (VNEG is *-3.3 V*). *Not* polarized. |
| =C20=, =C21= | 2.2 nF | *Ceramic* (C0G/NP0 nice, X7R OK) | Analog out filter shunt after 470 R on =OUTL=/=OUTR=. |

Do *not* put electrolytics on =C14=/=C15=. TI expects ~2.2 uF ceramics on the
charge pump; larger values can stress the pump.

* Shopping checklist (aggregated)

Buy at least:

- Ceramic 0.1 uF 0805 --- many (bypass)
- Ceramic 0.47 uF 0805 --- amp inputs + LM3940 Cin
- Ceramic 1 uF 0805 X7R --- MAX97220 charge pump + VDD bypass
- Ceramic 2.2 uF 0805 --- PCM5102 charge pump (=C14=, =C15=)
- Ceramic 2.2 nF --- DAC out filter (=C20=, =C21=)
- Ceramic 4.7 uF --- CP2102
- Ceramic or polarized 10 uF --- DAC bulk (=C12=)
- Ceramic 22 uF --- ESP / USB bulk (=C5=, =C30=)
- *Tantalum (or ESR-safe) 33 uF* --- LM3940 out (=C23=) ***do not skip ESR***

* PCM5102 clock mode (ESP RX) --- PLL vs "PCM"

These are different ideas:

1. *Audio data* --- You always send *I2S / PCM samples* from the ESP (=DOUT= ->
   DAC =DIN=, plus =BCK= / =LRCK=). Firmware is I2S master; format switch
   =FMT= low = I2S.

2. *System clock source* --- PCM5102 either:
   - *PLL mode* --- tie =SCK= to *GND*; chip makes its internal clock from
     =BCK=/=LRCK= (datasheet PLL operation).
   - *External SCK / non-PLL* --- drive a real system clock into =SCK=
     (needs an MCLK from the ESP or an oscillator).

*For this RX pack: use PLL mode* (=SCK= grounded). That matches the schematic
note and the usual ESP32 I2S master setup (BCK/LRCK/DOUT only; no MCLK pin in
=rx.c=). You are *not* choosing "PCM instead of PLL" --- you use *PLL for the
clock* and *I2S/PCM for the data*.

Refs: PCM5102 datasheet system-clock / PLL sections; ESP-IDF I2S std config
with =mclk = I2S_GPIO_UNUSED=.
